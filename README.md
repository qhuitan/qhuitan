I am PhD candidate in the Dartmouth MCB program, co-mentored by the [Curiel Lab](https://sites.dartmouth.edu/curiellab/) and the [McKenna lab](https://mckennalab.org/). My current areas of expertise include lineage tracing, cancer immunology, and transcriptomics (bulk and single-cell). I also have some experience working with Wnt signaling in _Drosophila_.

📦 __My R packages:__

* :chart_with_downwards_trend: {[ggbulksurv](https://github.com/qhuitan/ggbulksurv)} - Easy survival analysis for _Drosophila_ and _C.elegans_

📝 __My projects:__
* 🪰 {[otkwnt_embryo](https://github.com/harmstonlab/otkwnt_embryo)}: Bulk RNA-seq analysis of otk1, otk2 and wnt4 in _Drosophila_ embryo
* 🐭 {[deaf1](https://github.com/harmstonlab/Goh_et_al_deaf1)}: ChIP-seq analysis of Deaf1 in C2C12 mouse myoblasts
